Request 2 — Can we stop emailing the team's expenses?

Add named teams. Whoever creates one owns it and can add registered people by email as members or reviewers, change their role, and remove them. Owner is a separate role and cannot be removed or demoted through those member actions. People may belong to several teams. Owners/reviewers can read every expense in their team; ordinary members can read only their own team expenses. Everyone still has their personal ledger.

Expenses gain team_id (string or null) and status ('draft' for all current/new expenses). A creator who is currently in a team can move a personal draft into it, or move their team draft back to their own personal ledger. IDs and receipts stay the same. Only the creator can edit/delete a draft or replace/remove its receipt. Owners/reviewers read colleagues' drafts but do not edit them. A removed member loses team access; their team expenses remain there with original creator attribution. Personal expenses are unaffected.

Add browser team/member management and team expense browsing. GET /api/teams -> [{id,name,role}], POST with {name} -> 201 team (role owner); names are nonempty, at most 100 characters. GET /api/teams/{id}/members -> [{id,email,name,role}] for current members. Owner PUT to the same URL with {email,role:'member'|'reviewer'} adds/changes a registered person; DELETE .../members/{user_id} removes one (204). GET /api/expenses defaults to the personal ledger only; ?team_id={id} selects one team and returns the caller's visible expenses. Expense creation/PATCH accept team_id or null. Earlier personal-ledger behavior remains.
