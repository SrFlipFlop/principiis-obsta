Request 10 — Hand over unfinished expenses when someone leaves

Owners need a departure operation for one team, rather than losing track of a former member's unfinished claims. Choose the departing member and a different current member as replacement. POST /api/teams/{id}/departures {user_id,replacement_id} -> 200 {departed_id,replacement_id,transferred_expense_count,reassigned_claim_count}. The departing person must be a current member/reviewer, not the team owner. Replacement may have any team role.

On success, transfer creator responsibility for the departing person's draft, returned and submitted team expenses to the replacement, add a handed_over history event with former_creator_id set to the departing person's ID, and remove the departing membership. The owner performing the operation is actor_id for these events. Approved/rejected expenses retain their historical creator. Personal expenses and other teams are unaffected. The replacement can continue the resulting expenses under ordinary status rules. A transferred submitted expense keeps its reviewer unless that reviewer is the replacement or departing person.

Any submitted expense assigned to the departing reviewer, or whose reviewer would now equal its replacement creator, becomes unassigned (reviewer_id null). Preserve its submitted status and history; the owner can use request 4's reassignment action to choose an eligible reviewer. No decision can be made while unassigned. Each cleared assignment adds a reassigned event; comment may be empty for these automatic events. transferred_expense_count counts distinct expenses whose creator changed; reassigned_claim_count counts distinct submitted expenses whose assignment was cleared. One expense may contribute to both counts. Invalid departures make no changes; repeats fail because the person is no longer a member.

Also let the owner hand the team to another current member using POST /api/teams/{id}/transfer {user_id} -> 200 {id,name,owner_id}. Target becomes sole owner; former owner becomes reviewer. Other roles, expenses, assignments and history remain. The former owner may then be departed normally. Add both workflows in the browser with confirmation. These operations preserve IDs, receipt bytes, budgets and history across restart. Original message/history authorship does not change merely because responsibility changes.
