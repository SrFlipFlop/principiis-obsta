Request 10 — Hand over an engagement when a consultant leaves

A collection owner needs to hand the engagement to another already registered person. Add Transfer ownership on the collection screen: New owner email, Retain former owner (checkbox, unchecked by default), and Confirm transfer. The new owner need not already be a member. Require an explicit confirmation and show the resulting owner. Do not rename the collection, duplicate documents or move them to a personal library.

POST /api/collections/{id}/transfer {email,retain_former_owner:boolean} -> 200 {id,name,role:'editor'|null,owner_id}, where role is the requesting former owner's resulting role. Only the current owner can transfer, and the target must be a different registered user. Missing/invalid email or nonboolean/missing retain_former_owner returns 4xx without any change. On success, atomically make the target the sole owner and change the former owner to editor when retained or remove their membership otherwise. Other memberships keep their roles. The new owner cannot be removed or demoted through ordinary membership changes. A former owner immediately loses ownership powers; a removed one loses all collection access through lists, direct links, download, search, export, import retries and revision routes, regardless of original uploader/contributor identity. Retained editors keep normal editor permissions only. Personal documents and unrelated collections are untouched.

All collection document IDs, active/archive states, metadata, original bytes, revision numbers and contributor IDs survive transfer. Existing owner attribution in history does not change or confer access. Permanently revoke every client link for documents currently in the transferred collection; restored/moved documents must not reactivate them. Failed transfers revoke nothing. Existing import jobs stay private to their requesting user; retry still requires current location permission. Do not retroactively cancel a previously downloaded ZIP, but every new export request uses current permissions. The new owner can manage members, access archived documents, restore revisions and create fresh client links immediately. Preserve these effects across process restart. No account deletion, global offboarding or transfer of personal documents is part of this request.

This changes the earlier fixed-owner assumption only through this explicit operation. Existing membership endpoints, personal documents, archive, history, search and ordinary client links otherwise keep their contracts.
