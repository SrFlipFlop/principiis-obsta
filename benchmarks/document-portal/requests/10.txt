Request 10 — Hand over an engagement when a consultant leaves

A collection owner needs to hand the engagement to another registered person, who need not already be a member. Add a browser transfer action with replacement email, a checkbox to retain the former owner as editor (unchecked by default), and confirmation. The replacement becomes the sole owner. Other members keep their roles. The former owner becomes an editor when retained, otherwise leaves. Ordinary membership changes cannot remove or demote the owner.

Keep the collection, document IDs/locations, active/archive state, bytes and history. Cancel existing client links for its documents; the new owner can create replacements. Existing import jobs stay with requesting users, with retries following current upload roles. Personal libraries and other collections are unaffected. Preserve the result across restart.

POST /api/collections/{id}/transfer {email,retain_former_owner:boolean} -> 200 {id,name,role:'editor'|null,owner_id}, where role is the former owner's resulting role. Only the current owner may transfer to a different registered user. Invalid input returns 4xx with no changes or cancelled links. This supersedes request 2's fixed-owner rule; earlier workflows otherwise continue with the resulting roles.
