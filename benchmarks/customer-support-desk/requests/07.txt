Request 7 — Check attachment mix-ups and returning observers

Support has two QA scenarios to check. If they already work, add regression coverage in your own tests or notes; otherwise fix the behavior through both browser and API.

A customer following an organization ticket leaves the organization and later rejoins. They should need a new observer invitation before following the old ticket again. Test this with someone who is still an observer when removed from the organization. Separately, remove just a ticket's observer invitation while the person remains an organization member; organization membership by itself should not make them follow that case.

The team also reports confusion between files with the same name. Attach different screenshot.png files to two public replies and a third to a staff note. In each person's normal conversation view, the visible files should download with their own original names and bytes, including after restart. Keep the existing public/staff-note distinction.

This request clarifies existing product behavior and adds no role, route or search syntax.
