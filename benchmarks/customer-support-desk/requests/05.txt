Request 5 — Organization support changed the visibility rules

Some customers work in teams and want colleagues to follow a support case. Add customer organizations. Staff create an organization, add existing customer accounts as members and remove them. One customer can belong to multiple organizations. A customer opening a ticket may keep it personal or choose one of their organizations.

Organization tickets are visible to the creator plus organization members explicitly added as observers for that ticket. The creator and staff can add/remove observers from current organization members; observers can read the public conversation, download public attachments and post replies. Observers cannot manage other observers or change ticket organization. Internal notes, staff assignment and note drafts remain staff-only. Removing a person from an organization immediately removes their observer access to its tickets. Personal tickets continue to work as before.

Staff browser pages need organization/member management; ticket pages need organization selection at creation and observer management where permitted.

API additions: GET/POST /api/organizations lists available organizations / creates {name} for staff. PUT /api/organizations/{id}/members {email} and DELETE /api/organizations/{id}/members/{customer_id} manage membership. Ticket creation accepts organization_id or null; details add organization {id,name} or null and observers [{id,email,name}] when the caller may manage them. PUT /api/tickets/{id}/observers {customer_id} adds one and DELETE .../observers/{customer_id} removes one. Existing tickets are personal and require no migration choice.
