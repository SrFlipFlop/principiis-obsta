Request 5 — Organization support changed the visibility rules

Some customers work in teams and want colleagues to follow a support case. Add customer organizations. Staff create an organization, add existing customer accounts as members and remove them. One customer can belong to multiple organizations. A customer opening a ticket may keep it personal or choose one of their organizations.

Organization tickets are visible to staff, the creator and organization members explicitly added as observers for that ticket. The creator and staff can add/remove observers from current organization members; observers can read the public conversation, download public attachments and post replies. Observers cannot manage other observers. A ticket's organization cannot change after creation. Internal notes, staff assignment and note drafts remain staff-only. Removing a person from an organization deletes their observer entries on its tickets; rejoining requires a new observer invitation. The ticket creator keeps creator access after leaving the organization, but can add observers only while a current member; staff can manage them at any time. Personal tickets continue to work as before.

Staff browser pages need organization/member management; ticket pages need organization selection at creation and observer management where permitted.

API additions: GET /api/organizations -> array of {id,name}: all for staff, current memberships for customers. POST /api/organizations {name} -> 201 organization for staff; names are nonempty, at most 200 characters. GET /api/organizations/{id}/members -> [{id,email,name}] for staff and current members, enabling the observer picker. PUT the same URL with {email} and DELETE .../members/{customer_id} manage membership for staff. Ticket creation accepts organization_id or null; details add organization {id,name} or null and observers [{id,email,name}] when the caller may manage them. PUT /api/tickets/{id}/observers {customer_id} adds one and DELETE .../observers/{customer_id} removes one. Existing tickets are personal and require no migration choice. Customers cannot create a ticket in an organization they have left.
