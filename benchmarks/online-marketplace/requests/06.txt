Request 6 — Cancel before pickup preparation

We need a correction to the order lifecycle: either the buyer or that order's seller can cancel a pending order. A seller order is cancelled as a whole, independently of other sellers in the same checkout. Ready and collected orders cannot be cancelled. Cancellation returns its quantities to the corresponding listings exactly once, even if those listings are now draft. It does not reopen a cart or erase order history.

Add cancelled as a terminal order status. Repeating cancellation on a cancelled order returns the same cancelled order without another stock return. Cancel and mark-ready competing for the same pending order must yield one coherent result: either cancelled with stock returned, or ready with stock still allocated. Keep original subtotal, coupon and total on the cancelled record; no payment/refund action is needed because nothing was paid through the app.

Coupon use remains consumed after cancellation. This is deliberate: the promotion cap counts placed discounted orders, including subsequently cancelled ones. POST /api/orders/{id}/cancel -> 200 order for its buyer or seller. Add cancel controls to pending buyer/seller orders and show cancelled status in both histories. All other transitions and existing saved data keep working.
